    Settings

    Uses a pinned tab with the group's feed (right-click the tab → Pin). Pauses while your screen is locked or you're away, and skips a round while you're looking at that tab. While it's on, auctions you're bidding in are also re-read in the background every 15 min.
    A sale's Read button reads its lots and bids now, in a background tab that closes itself. Automatic re-reads use the same kind of tab, one post at a time.
    When a read finds you've been outbid, 10 minutes before a sale you're bidding in ends, and what you won or lost once it's over. Clicking one opens the lot on Facebook. Outbid and won/lost come from background reads, so auto-scan needs to be on for them to arrive by themselves.
    Under To pay, tick Send on the won auctions you want, then Send selected to inventory: it sends those wins (seller, lot, price, links, dates, the seller's shipping and payment terms, your Paid/Received marks) to your tcg_inventory, where they wait to be registered as orders. Nothing else is sent: no post reads, other people's names or comments.
    Kept on this computer only, and only as long as it's useful: a sale's post read (names, comments, bids) is deleted 7 days after it ends (30 days if you bid or claimed in it), posts not seen in the feed for 14 days go too, and so do Claude's answers nothing uses any more. Checked once a day. Running sales are never touched.
    Delete every stored post, post read and Claude answer? Your settings are kept.
